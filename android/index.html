Downloading Minima Bay for Android…

Finding the newest release.

If nothing starts, open the latest release and tap the .apk file.

Then

  1. Open the downloaded file and allow installing from your browser if Android asks.
  2. Have a Minima node app on your phone: Minima Core from the Play Store, or PandaBear or BlackBear.
  3. Open Minima Bay, then in your node app's Apps, switch Minima Bay on (and Admin, to sign). With more than one node app, choose yours in Minima Bay's Settings, Your node app.
  4. Pour test assets from The Faucet, and try The Rapids.
