Roadmap

Where Minima Bay comes from, and its three stages to real tokens: development, testing with valueless tokens, then production.

  1. May 2026

    It starts with The Pool

    An on-chain swap for Minima, straight from your wallet, with The Faucet's free test assets to try it.

  2. October 2026

    One app, and a fast order book

    Every app comes together in one install, with one Minima node and one wallet. The Rapids brings fast trading, settled on chain.

  3. Now · Stage 1 of 3

    Finalising development

    The apps are being finished and brought to one design across all six.

  4. Soon · Stage 2 of 3

    Testing with valueless tokens

    Every app is tested extensively with tokens that have no value, so anyone can try everything with nothing at risk. Join in.

  5. Finally · Stage 3 of 3

    In production with real tokens

    Once testing is complete, all six apps move to production and run with real tokens.
